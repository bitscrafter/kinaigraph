Making a video explainer means friction, whichever tools you reach for.
Motion is synced to narration by hand.
The work is labor-intensive and repetitive.
Nothing sits under version control, so you cannot see what changed.
Styling drifts from one video to the next.
Variations resist automation, and each one is built again.
The source moves, the video goes stale, and fixing it is heavy re-work.
And every language means authoring the whole thing over.
