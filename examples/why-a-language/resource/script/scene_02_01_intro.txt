Making a video explainer means friction, whether you reach for traditional tools or for AI.
Synchronizing motion with narration is a calibration you do by hand.
The work is defined by labor-intensive cycles and repetitive tasks.
Automated variations are hard to come by, and most iterations mean building it again.
Visual assets sit outside version control, detached from collaboration and history.
The source changes, the video goes stale, and fixing it means heavy re-work.
Styling drifts from one video to the next.
And every language means authoring the whole thing again.
